01 / About me

A student.
Always building.

I'm Sandeep. I moved from India to Nürnberg to study AI and robotics at the Technische Universität Nürnberg.

Most of my work starts with a fairly specific question. Does a deepfake detector still work after an image is compressed? Can a small model tell where a photo was taken? How has land cover changed around my city?

I like working through the whole process: preparing the data, training the model, checking where it fails, and building an interface someone can actually use.

Away from the screen, I like exploring new places and travelling. I'm usually the quieter person in the group.
